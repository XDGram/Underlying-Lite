# UIverse component references

The homepage adapts the following open-source UIverse patterns. Colors, sizing,
content, and motion were changed to fit Underlying's interface.

- Offset-shadow hover button by **felipesntr**: https://uiverse.io/felipesntr/yellow-robin-29
- Glow-border card by **alexruix**: https://uiverse.io/alexruix/heavy-elephant-39
- Rotating data loader by **Shoh2008**: https://uiverse.io/Shoh2008/nervous-lizard-11

The rest of the homepage and its issuer-to-company convergence animation are
original to Underlying Lite.
